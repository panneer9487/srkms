About Our School

A Legacy of Learning & Character

Founded in 2008, Sri Ramakrishna Matric Higher Secondary School is a private, unaided, co-educational institution in Arasampatti, Krishnagiri. Named after the revered sage Sri Ramakrishna Paramahamsa, we uphold the values of truth, service, and compassion — blending modern pedagogy with timeless wisdom to shape future-ready graduates.

Our Vision

To be a centre of holistic education that moulds students into responsible, compassionate, and globally competitive citizens.

Our Mission

To deliver quality education through dedicated teaching, modern infrastructure, and value-based learning for every child.

Curriculum

Academic Programmes

From kindergarten to higher secondary, our structured curriculum follows the Tamil Nadu State Board syllabus, designed to build strong foundations and critical thinking.

LKG – UKG

Pre-Primary Section

Play-based learning through structured activities, storytelling, and creative exercises. 343 enrolled students explore and discover in a safe, nurturing environment.

Class I – V

Primary Section

Strong foundation in Tamil, English, Mathematics, and Environmental Science. 791 students build core literacy and numeracy skills through engaging activities.

Class VI – VIII

Middle School

Introduction to Science, Social Studies, and advanced Mathematics. 323 students deepen subject knowledge and develop analytical and reasoning abilities.

Class IX – X

Secondary School (SSLC)

Comprehensive SSLC board preparation with 228 students. Focus on board exams, practical understanding, and academic confidence.

Class XI – Science

Higher Secondary – Science

Physics, Chemistry, Biology / Mathematics streams. Rigorous preparation for competitive entrances like NEET, JEE, and State boards.

Class XI – Commerce & Arts

Commerce & Arts Streams

Admission Process 2025–26

We welcome applications from students across all backgrounds. Admissions are open for LKG to Class XII for the academic year 2025–26.

1

Fill the Enquiry Form

Submit the online enquiry form below or visit the school office in person to collect the application form.

2

Document Submission

Submit the previous year's mark sheet, transfer certificate, birth certificate, and passport photo.

3

Interaction / Assessment

A brief interaction with the student and parent by the principal. For Classes VI and above, a basic aptitude check.

4

Fee Payment & Confirmation

Pay the admission fee at the school counter. Receive the confirmation letter and student ID.

Eligibility & Key Dates

  • LKG: Child must be 3+ years as of June 2025
  • Class I: Child must be 5+ years as of June 2025
  • Classes II–X: Previous year report card required
  • Classes XI–XII: SSLC / Class X mark sheet needed
  • Admission open: January – May 2025
  • Academic Year begins: June 2, 2025
  • Medium of instruction: Tamil & English
  • Co-educational institution
